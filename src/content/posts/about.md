# About me

I am Rahul, an AI engineer in Leeds. I have been building software for over 16 years.

I work in the trading department at Flutter UK & Ireland. I build AI tools that traders use every day, like automating Request a Bet and odds boosts, and a new experimentation platform with the data science team. I also started the AI Accelerator, which helps people across the department use AI in their own work. Before I moved into AI I spent three and a half years at Flutter as a software engineer.

## What I care about

- **Getting things into production.** A demo that works once is the easy part. The real work is what comes after it, like tests, monitoring, handling failures and keeping the running cost sensible.
- **Measuring it.** If you cannot show the model is helping, you do not know that it is. I write the evals before the features.
- **Turning research into real systems.** I read papers and use them in my work. The interesting part is usually what you have to change before they survive real traffic.
- **Products where mistakes matter.** Most of my career has been on large consumer platforms, where a bad decision reaches real customers fast.

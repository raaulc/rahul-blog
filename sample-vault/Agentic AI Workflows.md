Agentic AI is the shift from AI that answers questions to AI that takes actions. The difference matters.

## What makes a workflow agentic

- The model decides what to do next, not just what to say
- It calls tools, reads files, writes code, and loops until done
- The human is in the loop at the right level of abstraction

## Why this matters now

Models are good enough. The gap is tooling and workflow design.
